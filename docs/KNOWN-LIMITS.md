# Known limits

What is not proven, what is imperfect on purpose, and what to do about it, grouped by subsystem. Read this before go-live and whenever something behaves oddly. Nothing here is a secret.

How to read the tables:

- **UNVERIFIED-LIVE**: works against fakes in the offline tests but cannot be proven without the real instance or a live site. Each becomes a check in `docs/ACCEPTANCE.md`.
- **OPEN**: a known gap in the shipped code or process. **ACCEPTED**: a deliberate trade-off (docs/DECISIONS.md). **OWNER**: needs a decision or an action by the owner.
- `F<n>` is entry n (counting from 0) of the adversarial review of 2026-09-29/30 (112 findings), kept in the research folder as `_findings.json`. Findings that the build fixed are not listed here (they are summarised in
  docs/DECISIONS.md section 14); section 11 indexes every finding that was not fully fixed.
- Impact says what a person would see. Mitigation says what limits it today or what to check.
- The code state described here is the tree of 2026-09-30 at about 04:45 London. Re-check the OPEN rows against the code after any later change.
